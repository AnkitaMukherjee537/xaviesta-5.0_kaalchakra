KAALCHAKRA: THE WHEEL OF TIME

8th & 9th January 2027

Xaviesta is the annual B-school fest of Xavier Business School, St. Xavier’s University, Kolkata. Its fifth edition brings top institutes together for business competitions, sports and cultural performances, all turning on one question: what do you do when the moment moves on?

Tap a planet on the wheel to register for that arena.

Read the wheel. Lead the cycle.

Kaalchakra is the wheel of time: it turns, it repeats, and it rewards those who learn to read it. Markets shift, brands fade and the court clock runs down, and the winners are those who move with the wheel instead of against it. This is an edition where nothing resets and everything carries forward.

  • Bring your college ID card every day. Entry needs a wrist band from the registration desk.
  • Registration for each event closes half an hour before it starts.
  • You can enter many events, as long as the timings do not clash. Teams cannot mix colleges.
  • Prizes go to the winners and runners-up, announced at the closing ceremony.
